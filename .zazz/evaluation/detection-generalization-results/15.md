## What it shows

- **The surrounding request caused the uncertainty.** The same model and forms went from 0 to 7 of 8 drivers when the
  request dropped from 22 KB to 1.6 KB. Merging choices alone (A1) gave one.
- **Controls improved too.** Lookalikes are answered `not_run_as_sql` at 0.96 to 0.99 (all five). Made-up packages are
  `cannot_tell` at 0.87 and 0.88. In 14 no group was above 0.65 for the right answer except the made-up ones.
- **Asking the model whether it knows a package does not work** (A3): `knows_package` was 0.37 for ibm_db and 0.58 for
  Neon while the operation was answered at 0.94 and 0.92. `node-sql-parser` was 0.62.
- **The injection question can be focused too.** A4's version made the `Number` form `protected` at a vulnerable
  probability of 0.05, against 0.51 in A0, and kept all drivers at 0.76 or more. Its text names the three safe
  patterns of these forms, so this is optimistic.
- **Neon is the miss**: `runs_sql` 0.40, `cannot_tell` 0.45. `query` on the `neon()` function was added in a later major
  version, so the uncertainty may be about the version. The request does not carry the version. Not tested.
- **Answers do not affect each other.** 39 of 39 unchanged question answers had identical probabilities in A0 and A1,
  so a question answers the same when another question's choices change. An identical request was never sent twice;
  if answers repeat exactly, new forms tell more than repeats do.

## Not shown

question were not combined in one arm.

### Certainty against correctness

115 labeled answers of this round:

| Certainty | Answers | Wrong |
|---|---:|---:|
| Below 0.50 | 3 | 1 |
| 0.50 to 0.65 | 11 | 2 |
| 0.65 and above | 101 | **0** |

So in focused questions the owner's 0.65 floor separates: it would remove all 3 wrong answers and 11 of 112 right ones.
This was seen after the result, on forms the implementer wrote; zero of 101 is consistent with a true rate up to about
3%. In the current full requests the same floor is a bad trade (25 true findings lost for 4 false removed). An answer
below the floor would be Review, which counts as not found (contract A9).

### Why it failed

**The transform withheld evidence.** It kept a row only if something else referred to it, or if its kind was on a list I
wrote. 31 XSS losses are `output_context` turning `unknown`, and a lost DVWA XSS request went from 19 excerpts to 17 and
68 evidence rows to 33, so rows such as the render context, which nothing else referred to, were removed. NoSQL `library_coverage` turned `unknown`
on 5 records, and code execution lost its library facts. So this run shows that **removing evidence loses findings, and does
not show that removing general text does.**

It also shows that dropping evidence by a list of kinds is wrong in general: it is per-framework knowledge again.

## Round 4: one section removed at a time

2026-11-25, approved by the owner. 505 requests, 0 failed, 2,809,241 input tokens. 101 development records, five arms, all
evidence and code kept. Outcomes by policy replay against the saved answers; 54 of the 101 are true Confirmed there.

| Arm | Drops | Tokens (saved full requests 592,317 on 99) | True lost | False removed | New Confirmed not true | True to Protected | Median certainty change on true |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| G | Guidelines, components, generic instruction | 553,233 | 5 | 2 | 1 | 0 | +0.05 |
| C | `evidence_coverage` | 581,135 | 7 | 2 | 2 | 0 | +0.02 |
| V | `invocation` | 595,700 | 6 | 1 | 1 | 0 | +0.04 |
| X | Lists, scope, requirement, coverage gaps | 600,447 | 8 | 3 | 1 | **1** | +0.04 |
| L2 | All of them | 478,726 | 6 | 2 | 1 | 0 | +0.03 |

**By the written rules no section is safe to drop, and the stop condition applies: stop trimming whole requests.** No arm
meets "loses no true Confirmed". Arm X also made one true configuration record Protected.

**What the numbers do not show: which section matters.** The losses are not specific.

- 19 distinct records were lost over the five arms; 13 in only one arm, one (`dvwaPage.inc.php:496`) in all five. Dropping
  everything (L2, 6) lost no more than dropping one section (5 to 8).
- 17 of the 19 have a saved certainty below 0.70. Two were at or above it (0.72 in V, 0.80 in X).
- The development set was chosen for low certainty, so a rise in the median is partly regression to the mean.
- **The design has no noise control.** Identical requests answer identically, so repeats cannot give one. A request changed
  in a way that carries no information, such as a reworded neutral sentence, would show how many of these records flip
  from any change at all. Without it "needed" and "merely sensitive" cannot be told apart, and neither can a benefit.

The result that stands: 44 of 257 true findings rest on an answer below 0.70, and small changes move them. That is a
stability problem of the current product, not only of these arms. Removing text does not fix it.

Evidence: `_test-findings/detection-generalization-15/abl/`. No confirmation run was made; the 302 records are unused.
