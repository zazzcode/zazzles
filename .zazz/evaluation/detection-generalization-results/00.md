## 1. Draw

Seed `20261125` (Python `random.Random`), population sorted by file and line, 8 handlers from each of the five
Node development samples at the pins of `recall-gap-inventory/cases.json`. Equal shares were chosen because a
draw over the whole population would be more than half Juice Shop. Population: distinct HTTP handlers bound by
the scanner at `6ee8059`: juice-shop 100, nodejs-goof 18, vuln-demo-app 14, vulnerable-typescript-app 19, vuln-nextjs 35.

- Handlers the scanner does not bind are not in the population, so recall here is not recall over all entries.
- **The draw holds no `set_cookie`, `file_write` or `store_unknown` operation.** Cookie recall, which the stop rule
  names, cannot be measured on it. The draw was not repeated.
