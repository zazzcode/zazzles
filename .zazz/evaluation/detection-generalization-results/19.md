## Answer

- **Recording is solved in general.** The lab's vulnerable routes go from 0 of 57 recorded to 57 of 57, with one driver-specific
  line (`mongodb` to its query language). Nine samples: no record missing, added or changed.
- **Judging depends on the request, the question and the model.** Best measured: protocol v2 on Gemini gemini-decision-1,
  **52 of 57 vulnerable routes Confirmed, none Protected, 4 of 71 controls Confirmed: 93% of Confirmed routes are vulnerable.**
  Protocol v1: Gemini 50 and 7 (88%); gpt-6-luna 46 and 17 (73%). The series bar is 95%, so it is **close, and not met**.
- The lab is made of near twins. How the share holds in ordinary code is not measured.

## Sufficient source, the question, and a second model

Asked for by the owner: the issue is whether sufficient and correct information and questions reach the judge.

- **Audit.** 19 of check 2's 128 requests lacked the definition of a module-level name the handler uses (a constant map, a field
  list, a `JSON.parse` reviver, a module client); 6 of the 30 wrong answers were among them.
- **Check 6.** `referenced_definition` evidence adds those statements; 39 requests change. Moved: `desk/accept` and `desk/unlock`
  (controls) Confirmed to Review; `desk/renew` (vulnerable) Confirmed to Review; `desk/wave` (control) Review to Confirmed. Net
  zero, kept: the model should not judge code it is not shown.
- **Probe 4.** Rewording the question cleared the controls that select among listed alternatives (flagged 17 to 11) and cleared
  five more vulnerable routes, one at 0.98. Not built in.
- **Check 5, Gemini.** Identical evidence and questions. Vulnerable routes in Review: `sign-in/partner`, `sign-in/terminal`,
  `sign-in/portal`, `articles/entry`, `members/nickname`, `desk/context`, `loans/cancel`. Controls Confirmed: `articles/topic`,
  `members/settings`, `members/options`, `desk/load`, `parts/blend`, `people/finder`, `stats/export`. The two models agree on 106
  of 128 routes. Input tokens 226,039.

## Juice Shop and NodeGoat

- **Juice Shop** imports MarsDB, not `mongodb`. Its 11 NoSQL records (8 sendable) and all 305 request digests are unchanged, so its
  saved answers still apply. This work neither helps nor harms it.
- **NodeGoat**, before its source was opened: 1 entry and 0 records, on `stage` and on this work. The routes are registered on an
  app passed to another module, and handlers are methods of constructor-function objects. After the entry rules (S-5): 20 entries,
  30 records (7 NoSQL, 6 XSS, 3 code execution, 1 redirect, 13 validation).

| NodeGoat NoSQL record | Outcome | Read in source |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /login` `usersCol.findOne({ userName })` | Confirmed, operator 0.85 | True: JSON body object as `userName` |
| `POST /signup` `usersCol.findOne({ userName })` | Confirmed, operator 0.44 | True, same form; low impact |
| `GET /allocations/:userId` `find(searchCriteria())` with `$where` | Confirmed, expression_text 0.98 | True: the application's documented injection |
| `POST /memos` `memosCol.insert({ memo, timestamp })` | Confirmed, written_fields 0.50 | **False**: the request value is stored under a fixed key |
| `POST /profile`, `/contributions`, `/benefits` updates | Review, data_only 0.32 to 0.88 | Safe: `parseInt` selector, values under fixed keys |

## What this does not show

- No application keyed by another session before scanning. NodeGoat's labels are the implementer's, written after the run.
- No repeat of a request, no renamed copy. Gemini was run once, on the lab only.
- The other 23 NodeGoat records are not judged or read.
- The 0.95 floor for Protected was set after probe answers were seen.
- Capability pages, the analysis guide and the architecture page are not updated.
