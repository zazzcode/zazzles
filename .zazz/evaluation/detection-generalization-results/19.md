## Answer

- **Recording is solved in general.** The lab's vulnerable routes go from 0 of 57 recorded to 57 of 57, with one driver-specific
  line (`mongodb` to its query language). Nine samples: no record missing, added or changed.
- **Judging depends on the request, the question and the model.** Best measured: protocol v2 on Gemini gemini-decision-1,
  **52 of 57 vulnerable routes Confirmed, none Protected, 4 of 71 controls Confirmed: 93% of Confirmed routes are vulnerable.**
  Protocol v1: Gemini 50 and 7 (88%); gpt-6-luna 46 and 17 (73%). The series bar is 95%, so it is **close, and not met**.
- The lab is made of near twins. How the share holds in ordinary code is not measured.

## Sufficient source, the question, and a second model

Asked for by the owner: the issue is whether sufficient and correct information and questions reach the judge.

- **Audit.** 19 of check 2's 128 requests lacked the definition of a module-level name the handler uses (a constant map, a field
  list, a `JSON.parse` reviver, a module client); 6 of the 30 wrong answers were among them.
- **Check 6.** `referenced_definition` evidence adds those statements; 39 requests change. Moved: `desk/accept` and `desk/unlock`
  (controls) Confirmed to Review; `desk/renew` (vulnerable) Confirmed to Review; `desk/wave` (control) Review to Confirmed. Net
  zero, kept: the model should not judge code it is not shown.
- **Probe 4.** Rewording the question cleared the controls that select among listed alternatives (flagged 17 to 11) and cleared
  five more vulnerable routes, one at 0.98. Not built in.
- **Check 5, Gemini.** Identical evidence and questions. Vulnerable routes in Review: `sign-in/partner`, `sign-in/terminal`,
  `sign-in/portal`, `articles/entry`, `members/nickname`, `desk/context`, `loans/cancel`. Controls Confirmed: `articles/topic`,
  `members/settings`, `members/options`, `desk/load`, `parts/blend`, `people/finder`, `stats/export`. The two models agree on 106
  of 128 routes. Input tokens 226,039.

### Check 9: is the difference what is sent? Choice order

Compared byte for byte, the requests the two adapters send for one record have identical evidence, instructions and answer
descriptions. They differ only in the order of the answer choices: the Gemini adapter writes them alphabetically
(`cannot_tell`, `data_only`, … first), the OpenAI adapter in the written order (five "yes" answers first). Variant: OpenAI
choices sorted as Gemini's, so that both models receive the same bytes. 128 requests on gpt-6-luna, protocol v2.

| Same bytes, protocol v2 | Vulnerable Confirmed (57) | Vulnerable Protected | Controls Confirmed (71) | Share of Confirmed that is vulnerable |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Gemini `gemini-decision-1` (check 7) | 52 | 0 | 4 | 93% |
| OpenAI `gpt-6-luna` (check 9) | 43 | 0 | 10 | 81% |

**The gap is the model, not the request.** Order has a small effect on gpt-6-luna: against probe 4's raw answers in the written
order (46 called vulnerable, 11 controls flagged) the sorted order gives 43 and 10. The rule needed at least 46 Confirmed to
build the sorted order in; it is not built. The two models agree on 107 of 128 routes.

## Iteration loop on Gemini (307 requests)

A cached bench of the lab, NodeGoat, Juice Shop and nodejs-goof: 68 true records or routes, 80 safe. Each variant was a build
overlay, with its pass rule written first. Baseline, scanner `9d341f0`: 62 of 68 found, none Protected, 5 of 80 safe Confirmed.

| Variant | Result | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Mongoose and MarsDB records on the focused request | Juice Shop 6 of 7 found becomes 4 of 7; two nodejs-goof `findById` records fall from Protected to Review | No |
| Proven value facts in the request | Three controls become Protected; safe Confirmed 5 becomes 6 | No |
| The scanner's paths and stopping tests in the request | Its two target routes are found, two others are lost; lab safe Confirmed 4 becomes 9 | No |

- The 128 lab requests sent a second time gave 128 identical answers and probabilities: Gemini repeats itself here.
- Across four Gemini variants, 48 of 57 vulnerable lab routes are Confirmed by all and 59 of 71 controls by none. 7 vulnerable
  routes and 10 controls change with the wording; 2 controls are Confirmed by all (`articles/topic`, `people/finder`).
- From saved answers, not a run: confirming only where the committed request and the paths variant agree gives 50 found and
  3 false (94%).

### Agreement of two wordings (135 requests, 2026-11-26)

Rule written first: at least 95% and at least 59 of 68 found. A second wording of `request_control` with the same evidence, written
before its answers were seen. A record is Confirmed only when both wordings confirm it, Protected only when both protect it.

| Whole bench, Gemini | Found | True Protected | Safe Confirmed | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Committed wording | 62 of 68 | 0 | 5 of 80 | 92.5% |
| Second wording | 59 of 68 | 0 | 6 of 80 | 90.8% |
| Both agree | 58 of 68 | 0 | 4 of 80 | 93.5% |

**Fails both parts of the rule; not adopted.** Lab alone under agreement: 49 of 57 and 3 of 71 (94.2%). Lost: `members/nickname`,
`desk/pass`, `loans/cancel`, NodeGoat `/signup`. Still Confirmed under both: `articles/topic`, `people/finder`, `desk/load`,
NodeGoat `/profile`.

- The 0.95 floor for Protected was set after probe answers were seen.
- The published `vuln-nextjs` sample report is not regenerated.
