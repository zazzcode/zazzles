## Deferred to proposal 2 or owner decision

- Equality-only selector beyond Mongoose (MarsDB semantics unverified; B-JS-15).
- Argument-inert fixed executables such as `/bin/echo` (owner policy decision).
- Fixed origin from a service/config field (needs a no-writer proof; Juice
  `order-completion.component.ts:99`).
- Finite-allowlist ternary and negated-class strip (Juice `profileImageUrlUpload.ts:29`).
- `Buffer.toString('base64')` and quoted hbs attributes as encoders (needs schema-based typing).
- JSX ternary as URL-attribute guard, Python registry-element path selection, Python
  callable-parameter dispatch and `http.client` SSRF, local async helper return summary (PATH-N01).
- Same-origin SSRF comparison against a trusted base (no genuine corpus instance).
