## First failing stage

The model's answer to the call question. S-2 worked: `injection_protection` was `vulnerable` on 8 of 8 drivers, against
`unknown` on all 25 records of 03. S-1 raised the resolved calls from 2 of 16 in 03 to 4 of 8, on different forms, so the
two are not comparable as rates.

Read from the saved probabilities after the decision. Not evidence for a pass, and no rule may be built on them (D-6):

- No answer was confident. The highest probability of a running-SQL answer on any driver was 0.46.
- `executes` and `prepares_then_executes` split the same belief. Their sum was 0.29 to 0.64 on the eight drivers and 0.10
  to 0.24 on lookalikes and made-up packages, so the model does separate the groups.
- The call answer moves with the code around it: mariadb `execute` was `executes` in one form and `cannot_tell` in the
  `Number` form; Snowflake was `cannot_tell` in both.
- The `Number` answer was 0.51 `vulnerable` and 0.36 `protected`, with the scanner's verified-Number fact in the request.
  That is a false-positive risk of S-2 that a working call question would expose.
